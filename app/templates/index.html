About the numbers

Measured on held-out synthetic test receipts (546 images): 71% of forged receipts caught, 14% of authentic receipts wrongly flagged. "Confidence" is the observed accuracy for this score range, not a guarantee for your file. The model was trained on computer-made edits (replaced text, copy-move, splice, erase) and has not been shown to detect real-world forgeries. Use it as a screening aid, not as proof.
